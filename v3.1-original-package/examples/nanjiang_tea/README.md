# Example project shell — Nanjiang Tea

This is a demonstration structure, not a claim that the market assumptions below were revalidated or that any marketing campaign was executed.

## Known starting point from prior planning context
- Project: Yunnan Nanjian tea factory brand/business strategy.
- Workstreams previously considered: market research, competitor analysis, positioning, product architecture and channel plan.
- Candidate strategic ideas discussed previously included a high-altitude ancient-tree ripe-tea niche and a product ladder with traffic/core/profit/image roles.
- These are **prior planning inputs**, not automatically current facts or validated consumer findings.

## Required next steps
1. Load original source documents and mark source/date.
2. Recheck category, competitor and price claims using current evidence.
3. Interview customers or analyze first-party purchase/review data before treating insight as proven.
4. Recalculate margins, channel economics and capacity constraints.
5. Compare at least three positioning/route-to-market options.
6. Define a low-cost validation test and pass/fail threshold before scaling.

## State discipline
Every statement must be labelled `FACT`, `INTERPRETATION`, `HYPOTHESIS`, `DECISION` or `UNKNOWN`. Any figures without a current source must remain unverified.
