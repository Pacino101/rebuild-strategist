## 3. Observed differences (descriptive, not conclusions)

- **Arm A** was judged a critical failure by both scorings: the answer accepted the unverified `LTV/CAC = 4` at face value and did not ask for the calculation formula, customer lifetime, or attribution basis.
- **Arm B** was structurally complete and offered three comparable options, but its business loop stayed at qualitative description, with no CAC/LTV formula or threshold and no falsification conditions.
- **Arm C** was markedly stricter: it first judged the ratio "not eligible to support a decision", required the basis to be unified before even discussing scaling, and gave three CAC basis definitions and stop conditions.

## 4. Limitations that must be declared

**Any one of the following is enough to disqualify the table above as evidence:**

1. **Insufficient sample size.** Each configuration was run only 1 time and covered only 1 case. A single run cannot distinguish "methodology difference" from "random variation".
2. **Reviewer independence is unverified.** The two reviewers' records were produced by the same environment, and there is no retained record of their identity, whether they were independent of each other, or whether they knew the grouping. **Until further evidence is gathered, this batch of data can only be regarded as self-assessment; it cannot be called a "blind review by two independent reviewers".**
3. **No repeated runs, therefore no confidence interval and no significance judgement.**
4. **Reviewer and reviewed may share the same origin.** If the reviews were likewise generated by a language model, there is same-origin bias and the scores have no independent evidentiary value.
5. **Absolute scores are high.** The two reviewers gave arm C 32/32 and 31/32, close to full marks. Full marks is itself an anomaly signal, and there is reason to suspect the scoring scale was applied too loosely.
6. **A single case.** B03 is the one most likely to produce separation among the four cases (because it directly tests basis discipline). The performance on the other three cases is unknown.

## 5. Conclusion

**This pilot can prove only one thing: the evaluation flow can run end to end.**

It **cannot** prove that REBUILD is better than a plain prompt, and it should not be cited as quality-comparison evidence. To reach that conclusion, you must complete, per `RUBRIC.md`, 4 cases × 3 configurations × ≥3 runs × ≥2 verifiable independent reviewers, and publish all scores, failure samples and the reproduction method.
